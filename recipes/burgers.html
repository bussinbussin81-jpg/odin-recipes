Burgers

Description

Burgers are an American favorite:a juicy grilled beef patty on a toasted bun, topped with cheese,lettuce,tomato,onion and your favorite sauces.They're simple to make and easy to customize.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 is best)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar or American cheese
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • Ketchup, mustard or mayo
  • Pickles (optional)

Steps

  1. Divide the beef into 4 equal portions and shape each into a patty about 3/4 inch thick. Press a small dent in the center of each so they don't puff up while cooking.
  2. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a skillet or grill over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the patties for 4-5 minutes, flip them, and cook another 3-4 minutes until they reach 160F inside.
  5. Put a slice of cheese on each patty for the last minute and cover so it melts.
  6. Toast the buns cut-side down for 1 minute.
  7. Build the burgers with lettuce,tomato,onion,pickles and sauces.
