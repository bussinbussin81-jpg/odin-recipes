Tacos

Description

Beef tacfos are a Mexican classic: seasoned ground beef in crispy or soft tortillas, topped with lettuce,tomato,cheese and salsa. They're quick, easy to make and good for a weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning (or 1tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1/2 garlic powder, 1/2 tsp salt)
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 8 taco shells or small tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 diced tomato
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • Sour cream (optiona)

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 7-8 minutes, breaking it up as it cooks, until it's browned.
  2. Drain off the extra grease.
  3. Stir in the taco seasoning and water, then simmer for about 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  4. Warm the taco shells or tortillas in the overn or in a dry pan for a minute or two.
  5. Spoon the beef into the shells.
  6. Top with lettuce,tomato,cheese,salsa and sour cream.
